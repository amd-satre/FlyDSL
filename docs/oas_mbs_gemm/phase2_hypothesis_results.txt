=== Gaussian activation-like tensor  shape=(4096, 4096) ===
MXFP4-16 (no OAS):      QSNR =  18.95 dB
MXFP4-16 + OAS:         QSNR =  19.17 dB  (delta vs base: +0.21 dB)
MXFP4-16 + OAS + MBS-S: QSNR =  19.39 dB  (delta vs OAS: +0.23 dB)
MXFP4-16 + OAS + MBS-D: QSNR =  20.14 dB  (delta vs OAS: +0.97 dB)
Fraction of 16-blocks OAS bumps: 19.7%  (paper reports ~15%)

=== Outlier-heavy tensor (1% outliers @ ~25x scale)  shape=(4096, 4096) ===
